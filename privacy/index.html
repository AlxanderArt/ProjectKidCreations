PRIVACY NOTICE

Effective September 26, 2026 · Operator: PK Blick · Owner: Aíel

Launch age boundary

Browsing is public, but account onboarding is limited to people age 18 or older during launch. We do not currently offer a guardian-consent workflow. If you are under 18, do not submit personal information or create an account.

Information collected at launch

  • Phase One: name, email address, project interests, and optional message.
  • Email verification and onboarding: verification status and submission identifiers.
  • Profile setup: display name, username, optional bio, skill level, and gear interests.
  • Account security: password hash handled by the account system, session records, security events, and account activity.

We do not collect a birth date, social-media handles, shipping address, payment card details, or public profile content during launch.

How information is used

  • To verify onboarding, create and secure an account, and provide requested account features.
  • To respond to questions and operate, troubleshoot, and protect the service.
  • To send optional product updates only when a person actively opts in.

Profiles are not publicly displayed during launch. Submissions are not automatically published. ProjectKidCreations does not sell personal information.

Service providers and access

ProjectKidCreations uses service providers for website hosting, workflow automation, email delivery, and operational records. These providers process information only to provide those functions. Access is limited to the operator and authorized service systems.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Information is kept only while needed to operate the requested account, maintain security records, resolve support issues, or meet legal obligations. Account deletion and data-access requests can be made using the contact below. Records that must be retained for security or legal reasons may be kept only for that limited purpose.

Marketing is off by default. It can be declined during onboarding or changed from the account settings.

Contact

For privacy, access, correction, or deletion requests, email projectkidcreations@gmail.com.
